* **Evidence in relation to Suspects**
  * Exculpatory Evidence
    : It is used to prove the innocence of suspects.
  * Inculpatory Evidence
    : It is used to prove the guilty of suspects.
* **Evidence by Its Characteristic**
  * Class Evidence
    : It shows the common traits of a group of objects, so it cannot identifies a single object.
    : &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Ex) operating systems
  * Individual Evidence
    : It shows the identifiable traits of a single object.
    : &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Ex) IP address
